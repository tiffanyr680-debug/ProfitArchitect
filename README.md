# 📊 ProfitArchitect PRO 

**Stop the Profit Bleed. Reclaim Your Business Margin.**

ProfitArchitect PRO is a local-first financial intelligence engine designed for modern founders, agency owners, and e-commerce entrepreneurs. We help you find hidden waste in your bank data without ever risking your privacy.

## 🚀 How It Works

Unlike QuickBooks or Xero, ProfitArchitect doesn't require a login or cloud connection. 

1. **Local-First Processing:** Users upload their Bank CSV directly into their browser.
2. **Logic Engine:** Our Javascript-based engine scans transaction patterns for "Vampire Subscriptions" and recurring non-essential costs.
3. **Margin Intelligence:** The app instantly calculates Net Profit Margin and Tax Liability (based on 2026 tax standards).
4. **ROI Mapping:** If a user's margin is below 30%, the app triggers a "Recovery Session" offer to help them optimize their business.

## 🔒 Security & Privacy

Privacy is our product. 
- **Zero Transmission:** We never send your financial data to a server.
- **Memory Only:** Analysis happens in the browser's RAM and is destroyed when the tab is closed.
- **No API Keys Required:** This tool is 100% self-contained logic.

## 💰 The Business Model

ProfitArchitect PRO is a high-ticket "Foot-in-the-door" tool. 

- **The Hook:** A free, private profit audit.
- **The Service:** $1,000 "Profit Recovery Session" where we fix the issues identified by the engine.
- **The Upsell:** Ongoing "Profit Fractional CFO" services starting at $500/mo.
