Quick Start Guide

1. Export Bank Data

Log in to your business bank account. Go to "Transactions" and export your last 3 months as a CSV file.

2. Run the Scanner

Open ProfitArchitect and drag your CSV into the uploader. Our engine will automatically tag "Vampire Subscriptions" and tax liabilities.

3. Book Recovery

If your margin is below 30%, click "Book Recovery" to schedule a 1-hour session to automate your bookkeeping and reclaim your profit.
